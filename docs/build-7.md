# Build 7: one box, one button

The owner rated Build 6 at 3 out of 10. An independent reviewer then read every screen's
code with instructions to judge it harshly. Its verdict: the 3 was fair. The app was "a
faithful port of a website's control panel, not a phone product": every option, price and
caveat on screen at once, all in the same boxed style. Build 7 acts on that review.
Not compiled or run yet.

## What changed

| Screen | Before | Now |
|--------|--------|-----|
| Create | Language switch, four source tabs, three numbered steps, up to 22 controls | A title "What will you say?", one script box, two buttons under it (Speak, Write it for me), the voice, More options, and recent scripts. One lime button pinned at the bottom with its price |
| The Amharic / English switch | A big switch at the top, right after choosing the app language | Gone from the main screen. The voiceover language starts as the app language and lives in More options |
| English-only extras | Reading speed and emphasis shown on the main screen, only for English | Reading speed is in More options and only appears for English. Emphasis is offered after the voiceover is made |
| Write it for me | Three separate tabs; a link took three steps | One sheet with three choices: from an idea, from English text, from a link. A link is fetched and written in one go |
| No voice yet | "Step 2", under the script | The first thing on the screen, and the bottom button says "Record my voice" |
| Errors | Shown in step 1 while the button was at the bottom, so it looked like nothing happened | Shown right above the button that was tapped |
| Voiceover | Player, video, share, the whole fixing tool and its price notes on one screen | Player, "Make a video", "Share or save", and one row: "Something sound wrong?" |
| Fixing | Mixed into the voiceover screen | Its own screen, with two large choices, your list of changes, and one pinned button "Apply N changes · 1.5 credits" |
| Make a video | A technical question first (stock or own footage), two buttons each with a paragraph | Two tiles drawn as a phone and a TV, Captions (on) and Music (first track), and one button "Make my video" with the total price. Own clips, the presenter and "Review the shots first" are under More options |
| One-tap video | Made with no captions and no music | Uses captions and music |
| After a video | No way back to the shots; failed and stopped had no button at all | "Change shots and make it again" and "Back to the shots" |
| Look | Every box had a border and a hard shadow; grey system switches | One lime "hero" card per screen keeps the brand border and shadow. Everything else is a flat grey surface. Second buttons are white with an outline. Switches are black with the Kinbir font. Moving sound bars while a voiceover is made |

## Bugs the review found, now fixed

- A typed pronunciation had to be 3 letters; many Amharic words are 2.
- After changes were sent, the list stayed and a second tap could pay twice.
- An English script in an Amharic voiceover (or the reverse) was sent anyway. The app now says so before any credits are spent.
- One-tap video forgot it was one-tap if the app was closed during planning.
- Several lines stayed in English in the Amharic interface.
- "Arrives in the next build" was developer talk. It now says "coming soon".

## Not done from the review

- A recording screen with a countdown and sound meter.
- Thumbnails in the Library (the server would need to send pictures).
- An Amharic typeface with several weights, so Amharic headings look as strong as English ones.
- Reading speed for Amharic (the server supports it only for English).
- Illustrations for empty screens.
