# Test checklist (Build 1 and Build 2)

Things only you can check, because they need a real phone, real email and your real voice.
Write down what happens for each, and screenshot anything odd.

## 1. Welcome

- Open Kinbir. Do you see the lime logo and "Amharic voiceovers in your own voice"?
- Tap **Play in the Kinbir voice**. Do you hear the sentence? How long did it take?
- Change the sentence and play again.

## 2. Sign in

- Tap **Start free**. Enter your email. Tap **Send me a code**.
- Did the email arrive? How long did it take?
- Enter the code. Does the keyboard offer the code from your email?
- Are you taken to the Create screen?

## 3. Your voice

- Use your normal Kinbir email. Do your existing voice and credits show?
- Try a second, new email. Do you see **Clone your voice first**?
- Tap **Clone my voice**, then **Record now**. Does the front camera open?
- Read the script and stop. Does it upload and say **Your voice is ready**?
- Do the free credits show at the top of the Create screen?

## 4. Script

- Type a few lines of Amharic. Does it say **Amharic script**?
- Clear it. Paste a paragraph of English. Does it say **English text** and show a cost?
- Tap **Write Amharic script**. Does the Amharic appear as it is written?
- Tap **Undo**. Does the English come back?
- Paste a link to a news article. Tap **Get the article**. Does the text appear?

## 5. Voiceover

- With an Amharic script, tap **Make voiceover**.
- How long until the player appears?
- Tap play. Is it your voice? Is the sound clear?
- Drag the bar. Does it jump to that point?
- Tap **Share or save**, then **Save to Files**. Is the file there and does it play?
- Does the credit number go down by the right amount?

## 6. Library and Account

- Open **Library**. Is the voiceover listed? Tap it. Does it open and play?
- Open **Account**. Are your email and credits right?
- Open **My voices**. Can you rename a voice? Can you switch voices?
- Tap **Sign out**. Are you back at Welcome? Sign in again.

## 7. Look and feel

- Is any text cut off or overlapping?
- Is any Amharic text squeezed or hard to read?
- Does anything look different from the Kinbir website colours?
- Is anything confusing or more steps than it should be?

## 8. Video (Build 2)

- On a finished voiceover, tap **Make a video**. Keep Vertical and Stock. Tap **Plan the shots**.
- How long does planning take? Do the shots appear with pictures?
- Do the pictures match what the words say?
- Tap a shot. Tap a different clip. Does the shot change?
- In a shot, type other words and search. Do new clips appear?
- Choose music and tap **Listen**. Do you hear it?
- Tap **Make video**. How long until it is ready?
- Does the video play in the app? Is the voice in sync with the clips?
- Tap **Save or share video**, then **Save Video**. Is it in Photos?
- Open **Library**. Is the video listed?
- Make another video with **Only mine**. Add two or three of your own clips. Do they upload and appear on the shots?
- If you ever see **Stock footage is not available right now**, add your own clips. Do the empty shots fill?

## Do not test yet

- **Delete my account** really deletes it. Only try it with a throwaway email.
- Buying credits is not in these builds.
